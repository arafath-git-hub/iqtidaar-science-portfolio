02 / KNOWLEDGE DATABASE

Space Explorer

Explore a few topics that inspire my curiosity about space.

☀ THE SUN

The star at the centre of our solar system. Its gravity holds the planets in orbit.

MERCURY

The smallest planet and the closest planet to the Sun.

VENUS

A rocky planet with a very dense atmosphere, mainly composed of carbon dioxide.

EARTH

Our home planet, with liquid water on the surface and life as we know it.

MARS

A rocky world known for its reddish surface and evidence of ancient water.

JUPITER

The largest planet in our solar system and a gas giant.

SATURN

A gas giant famous for its extensive ring system.

URANUS

An ice giant that rotates with an extreme axial tilt.

NEPTUNE

A distant ice giant known for powerful winds.

WHAT IS A STAR?

A star is a massive, luminous ball of hot plasma. Most stars generate energy through nuclear fusion in their cores.

THE SUN

The Sun is the star closest to Earth. It is classified as a G-type main-sequence star.

GALAXIES

Galaxies are enormous systems of stars, gas, dust and dark matter held together by gravity.

MILKY WAY

Our solar system is part of the Milky Way, a barred spiral galaxy.

NEBULAE

Clouds of gas and dust in space. Some are regions where stars form.

BLACK HOLES

Regions of spacetime where gravity is so strong that nothing, not even light, can escape from inside the event horizon.

COMETS

Icy bodies that can develop glowing comas and tails when they approach the Sun.

EXOPLANETS

Planets that orbit stars beyond our solar system.
